EditorialCase study 01 of 09

The Night Shift

Client
Harbor Weekly
Year
2025
Format
Full page + 3 spots
Timeline
9 days

The brief

Harbor Weekly's winter issue ran a long feature on the people who keep the city going after midnight: bakers, night nurses, bus drivers, security staff. The art director wanted an opener that felt warm rather than lonely, and that would still read at thumbnail size on the magazine's homepage.

My role

Concept, three sketch routes, final full-page illustration and three small spot drawings for the online edition. Typography by the magazine.

The outcome

Ran as the section opener and became the issue's most shared story online. The spot set was licensed a second time for the magazine's podcast artwork.

EditorialCase study 02 of 09

Inbox Zero, Mind Full

Client
Ledger & Lamp
Year
2025
Format
Web header + social crops
Timeline
5 days

The brief

An essay on email overload for a business magazine that publishes mostly online. It needed one image that worked as a wide article header, a square social post and a tall story crop, without anything important falling off the edge.

My role

Illustration built on a flexible grid so every crop keeps the head and the flying envelopes. Delivered layered files so the web team could animate the envelopes.

The outcome

The animated header ran for two weeks on the homepage. The editor has since commissioned a quarterly series in the same style.

EditorialCase study 03 of 09

The Seed Library

Client
Common Ground Quarterly
Year
2024
Format
Cover + 4 spots
Timeline
12 days

The brief

A community gardening quarterly devoted an issue to neighbourhood seed libraries, where people borrow seeds and return them after harvest. The cover had to be hopeful, a little funny, and printable in three spot colours on uncoated paper.

My role

Cover illustration, four spot drawings for the inside pages, and separated colour files for the riso printer.

The outcome

The issue sold out its print run of 2,000. The quarterly now sells the cover as a poster, with a share of sales going to the libraries featured.

Book coversCase study 04 of 09

The Salt Orchard

Client
Tidewater Press
Year
2025
Format
Hardback jacket
Timeline
4 weeks

The brief

A debut novel about three sisters who inherit a lemon grove on an eroding coastline. The publisher wanted a cover that stood out on a crowded front table and hinted at the book's mix of family comedy and quiet grief.

My role

Cover illustration and hand-set title lettering, spine and back cover layout, plus a square crop for the audiobook edition.

The outcome

Picked up by two bookshop chains for front-of-store display. The publisher reused the lemon motif for the paperback's marketing and tote bags.

Brickyard Books
Year
2024

CharactersCase study 06 of 09

Pip & Ferro

Client
Tinker Town
Year
2025
Format
Mascot system
Timeline
6 weeks

The brief

Tinker Town is a coding app for children aged six to ten. They needed two guide characters that could explain ideas, celebrate small wins and react when something goes wrong, without ever feeling like a telling-off.

My role

Character design, model sheets, 24 expressions and poses, and a simple animation guide for the in-house team.

The outcome

Pip and Ferro launched with the app redesign and now appear on every screen. Lesson completion rose after the characters replaced text-only hints.

Bramble Books
Year
2024

MuralsCase study 08 of 09

Tide Lines

Client
Eastgate Community Pool
Year
2025
Format
14 × 4 m interior wall
Timeline
3 weeks on site

The brief

The pool reopened after a renovation and wanted its long entrance wall to feel like a summer day, even in January. The mural had to survive humidity and chlorine, and be readable from the far end of the pool hall.

My role

Mural design, colour specification in pool-safe paint, and painting on site with one assistant over three weeks.

The outcome

The wall is now the backdrop for swim-club photos and birthday parties. The council has asked for a matching design for the outdoor lido.

MuralsCase study 09 of 09

Market Day

Client
Pine Street Food Hall
Year
2024
Format
9 × 3 m feature wall
Timeline
8 days on site

The brief

A new food hall with twelve independent traders wanted a mural that said fresh and local without favouring any one stall. It sits above the shared seating, so it had to work behind a crowd of diners.

My role

Mural design, a small set of matching menu icons for the traders, and painting on site over eight evenings.

The outcome

The mural appears in most photos tagged at the hall. The traders use the matching icons on their menus and loyalty cards.
