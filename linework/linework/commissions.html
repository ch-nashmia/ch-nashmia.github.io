    04 FAQ

    Good questions, straight answers

    Anything missing? Email hello@example.com and I'll add it here.

    Who owns the artwork?

    I keep the copyright and you get a licence for the use we agree in the quote: an editorial one-off, all editions of a book, a campaign, merchandise and so on. If you need to own it outright, a full buyout is available and priced separately.

    How many revisions are included?

    You get three sketch routes, one round of changes on the chosen sketch and one round of small tweaks on the final. Most projects never need more. Extra rounds are quoted before I start them, never added to the invoice by surprise.

    How does payment work?

    A 50% deposit books your slot in the calendar and the balance is due on delivery of final files. Murals are split 40/40/20 across design sign-off, start of painting and completion. I invoice in US dollars, euros or pounds.

    Can you work to a rush deadline?

    Often, yes. Spot illustrations can turn around in 48 hours when the calendar allows, with a 25% rush fee. Pick a date in the brief form and I will tell you straight away whether it is realistic.

    Do you travel for murals?

    I paint anywhere within a day's drive of the studio and travel further for larger walls, with travel and lodging quoted separately. Every mural starts with a site visit or a video call and photos of the wall.

    What files will I receive?

    Print-ready PDF and TIFF at the final size, web-ready PNG and JPG crops, and for characters and murals the layered source files. Everything arrives in one shared folder, clearly named.
